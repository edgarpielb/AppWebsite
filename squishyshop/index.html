What’s in the game

Squeeze and stretch

Press, poke or stretch with two fingers. Jelly wobbles and foam rises slowly back. On iPhone, you feel it too.

Decorate your house

Four rooms and a deck to fill. Taps run, lamps light up and the toast pops out of the toaster.

Open a surprise box

Earn coins by playing, then open boxes to find dumplings, boba, sushi, ice cream and more.

Trade with Bao

Buy from Bao, sell him your spares and swap on his trading board. He is always fair. Mostly.

Fill your album

Over 60 squishies in six sets, and some of them are very rare.

Create characters

Make the kids who live in the house and dress them up, from their hair to their shoes.

Play 8 mini-games

Each one waits on the lawn, and every game pays coins for more boxes.

  • Boba Pour
  • Squish Jump
  • Squish Match
  • Squish Blocks
  • Squish Slide
  • Sort the Shop
  • Spot the Squishy
  • Steamer Stack

For grown-ups

Made to be handed to a child.

  • No ads, no accounts, no chat. And nothing to type.
  • Collects no data about your child. Read the privacy policy.
  • Plays offline. The collection is saved on the device and backed up to your own iCloud.
  • Coins are never sold. They are earned by playing, and boxes cost coins only.
  • Every Album squishy can be won. Nothing in the main Album needs money.
  • Purchases ask a grown-up first. Each is a named item at its shown price.
