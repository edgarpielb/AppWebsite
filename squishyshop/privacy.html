Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026
The short version

Squishy Shop collects nothing about your child. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party code of any kind in the app. Your child’s collection lives on their device.

01

What we collect

Nothing.

We do not collect names, email addresses, photos of people, birthdays, locations, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any free text. The app contains no text field a child could type into, and no way to send a message to anyone.

02

What is kept on the device

The game keeps its own save on the device: the collection, the room, coins and tickets, settings, and game progress and scores. It also keeps a list of recent coin and ticket changes, which the app never sends anywhere or copies to iCloud. When the app is first opened it makes a random number, used only to choose the shop’s goods, the squishies on the trading board and the squishies on the shop’s lower shelves each day. It is not linked to you, your child or the device, and it is never sent to us.

03

What leaves the device

Two things, and neither goes to us.

Purchases. If you buy a squishy, a decoration pack or a house for the yard, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details, your name, or your Apple Account. The app receives only Apple’s confirmation, on the device, that a purchase completed.

Your own iCloud backup. The save, including that number, is mirrored to the iCloud account signed in on the device, so a new phone or iPad restores your child’s collection. That copy is in your iCloud account, not ours. We cannot read it. If you sign out of iCloud, the mirror stops and the save on the device is unaffected. If the app is deleted and installed again, the iCloud copy can bring the collection back, as long as the device has synced with iCloud before the game is opened.

04

Third parties

There are none. Squishy Shop contains no analytics library, no crash reporter, no advertising network, and no social media kit. It uses only frameworks supplied by Apple. We have no servers, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

05

Advertising

There is no advertising in Squishy Shop, of any kind, including advertising for our own other apps.

06

In-app purchases

Squishy Shop is free to play and every squishy in the game can be earned without spending money. Purchases are optional and buy a named squishy, a decoration pack or a house for the yard, each at the price shown in your own currency.

Every purchase sits behind a parental gate: a multiplication question, answered on large on-screen digit buttons, asked again each time the app is opened. Apple’s own Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls work normally on top of this, and Screen Time’s In-App Purchases switch turns purchasing off entirely.

Real money never buys a random outcome. Mystery boxes are opened with coins earned by playing, and each box’s chances are one tap away, on the same page as the button that opens it. Money only ever buys a specific named item you can see before you buy it, and the game has no currency that is sold for money.

07

Children

Squishy Shop is made for children aged 8 and up and is published in the App Store Kids Category. Because we collect no personal data at all, there is nothing to consent to under GDPR, GDPR-K, COPPA or the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and nothing for us to delete on request.

08

Photos

The photo feature saves a picture of your child’s room to your device’s photo library. It asks for permission to add photos, and only to add them, the first time it is used. It never reads your existing photos and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

09

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. If we ever started collecting anything, which we do not plan to, we would say so here first and in the app.
