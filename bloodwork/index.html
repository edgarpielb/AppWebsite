Step 1: Import
Share a lab report

Send a PDF to Bloodwork from Mail or Files and it reads the results table: values, units, and the reference ranges your lab printed.

Step 2: Review
Confirm every value

Nothing is saved silently. Each import opens a review screen where uncertain readings are flagged instead of hidden, and you approve or correct each row before it enters your history.

Features

The whole picture,
without the guesswork

Built for people who get blood drawn more than once and want to see what changes between the printouts.

📄
Lab PDF Import

Share a report straight from Mail or Files. Values, units, and reference ranges are read for you, all on your device.

✅
Review Every Import

Every import passes through a review screen first. Uncertain readings are flagged for your attention, never silently dropped.

✍️
Manual Entry

Got a paper report or a value read over the phone? Type it in and it joins the same timeline.

📈
Trends Over Time

Each marker is charted against the reference range your own lab printed, so you see change in its proper context.

📚
Marker Encyclopedia

Plain-language explanations of what each marker measures and why labs test it, from hemoglobin to thyroid hormones.

🧭
No Verdicts

Bloodwork shows what your lab reported and forms no judgement of its own. Interpretation stays where it belongs: with your doctor.

🩺
Doctor Visit Prep

Gather the results you want to discuss and the questions you want to ask, then walk in with one clean page instead of a folder of printouts.

📤
Exports That Mean It

A one-page PDF summary for your doctor, and a complete CSV and JSON export of everything for you. Your data is never locked in.

☁️
Private iCloud Sync

Syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account. No account to create, and no server of ours anywhere in the chain.
